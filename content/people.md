# Key People

<p class="lead">The network has {人與機構數} nodes. These thirteen are the ones the paper, the poster and the <a href="history.html">Historical Context</a> page talk about.</p>

The paper anonymizes individual nodes (Arr. #1, Eng. #2) except for CHEN Chih-yuan 陳志遠. The poster adds portraits for the people who also appear in the Taipei Music Center's oral-history archive. Here they are named, grouped by the four arrangement traditions of Figure 3, with a short biography, their position in our analysis, and a link to their profile and filmed interview at the Taiwan Popular Music Database. Two of the thirteen have no profile in the database and are shown without a portrait.

Names are written surname first, as they are spoken, with the surname in capitals: CHEN Chih-yuan 陳志遠. The group headings keep the paper's labels for the four traditions. English stage names, where they exist, are given alongside. Biographies are paraphrased from the database and from the first author's dissertation, not copied.

## Not pictured, but in the network

The paper's military-club cohort also includes the guitarist YU Cheng-yen 游正彥 (b. 1952) and the bassist KUO Tsung-shao 郭宗韶 (1950–2017), who with HUANG Jui-feng and CHEN Chih-yuan formed the rhythm section known in the trade as the “Gang of Four” 四人幫; the engineer HSU Chung-hsien's partner at Lifeng Studio, WANG Chia-tung 王家棟 (b. 1951); and the arranger LIN Kuo-hsiung 林國雄 of the Electone group.
